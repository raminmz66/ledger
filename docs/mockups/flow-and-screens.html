Calm notebook style reused from the asset tracker. Shown at phone size. The Persian text is the real copy.

Flow

ورود با ایمیل← کد تأیید← خانه: فهرست اشخاص← صفحه‌ی شخص← فرم تراکنش

From Home: «+ شخص جدید» opens a name sheet, and ⚙ opens Settings (email, sign out). On a person's page: tap a transaction to edit or delete it; ⋯ lets you rename or delete the person.
